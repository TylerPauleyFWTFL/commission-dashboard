For affiliates and coaches

See what you've earned — and what's missing

Load the commission report from your affiliate or partner portal and get a clear picture of your earnings, plus a check for the things that are easy to miss: clients who quietly stopped paying, months that never paid out, and money taken back.

Choose your report

Or drag the file here. It's the spreadsheet file (CSV) you download from your portal.

Your file stays on your device. Nothing is uploaded, sent, or saved anywhere else. Your clients' details never leave this screen.

What you'll see

  • What you've earned — totals, your best month, and how each month compares.
  • Clients who stopped paying you — the regulars who have gone quiet.
  • Months that never paid — a membership that kept going but skipped a payout.
  • Money taken back — refunds and corrections, and what they cost you.
  • Payments that failed — clients you can still keep, if you reach out now.
  • Anything you may be owed — with a ready-written message you can send to ask.
